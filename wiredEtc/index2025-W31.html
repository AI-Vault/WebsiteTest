Google's new AI model, AlphaEarth Foundations, is designed to monitor and analyze climate change by acting as a "virtual satellite." This machine learning tool utilizes terabytes of satellite data to create detailed, color-coded maps of Earth's features, such as vegetation, water sources, and human constructions. It aims to provide insights into how ecosystems function and how factors like air quality and groundwater fluctuate. The system has already successfully detailed complex terrains in Antarctica and agricultural variations in Canada. Collaborating with entities like MayBiomas, AlphaEarth reduces the overwhelming volume of data while improving accuracy—reportedly 23.9% more accurate than similar models. While not consumer-facing, AlphaEarth will enhance Google Earth Engine and is designed to respect privacy, not capturing identifiable individual features. The initiative reflects Google's commitment to providing actionable data for ecosystem preservation and infrastructure planning. link
