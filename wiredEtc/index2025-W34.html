Chinese ‘Virtual Human’ Salespeople Are Outperforming Their Real Human Counterparts (.wired:20-8-2025)

Chinese ecommerce platforms are increasingly using AI-generated "virtual humans" as salespeople, outperforming human counterparts in sales. These AI avatars, developed by companies like PLTFRM using technology from Baidu and DeepSeek, operate around the clock to promote and sell products on platforms like Taobao and Pinduoduo. They can engage with audiences in real-time, answering questions and providing product details, which has led to significant sales increases—up to 30% in some cases. Despite concerns about job displacement for human salespeople, the technology currently complements rather than replaces them. The advent of more realistic avatars with improved language capabilities has transformed livestream ecommerce, where a significant portion of sales now occurs. Meet Wukong, the AI Chatbot China Has Installed on Its Space Station (.wired:21-8-2025)

China has introduced Wukong, an AI chatbot, aboard its Tiangong space station to enhance safety and coordination during missions. Named after the “Monkey King” from Chinese mythology, Wukong AI was designed from a domestic open-source AI model and specializes in navigation and tactical planning. Since its activation in mid-July, it has assisted taikonauts during spacewalks and routine inspections, offering comprehensive support through a dual-module system that connects the station's operations with ground analysis. While not the first AI in space, Wukong uniquely merges intelligent assistance with aerospace navigation. This development aligns with China's ambitions to solidify its space power status and expand the Tiangong station into a logistics hub for future lunar missions. Wukong symbolizes adaptability and the pursuit of knowledge, reflecting its purpose in aiding complex space operations effectively. link
