The Cleveland Clinic and the startup Piramidal are collaborating to create an AI model that utilizes electroencephalogram (EEG) data to monitor brain health in ICU patients. This AI system aims to continuously interpret EEG data, identifying abnormalities much faster than current manual analyses, which can take several hours. The model draws from a diverse dataset, nearly a million hours of EEG data, allowing it to adapt to individual brain activity patterns. Initially, the model will undergo tests in a controlled ICU environment, with plans for wide-scale implementation aimed at enhancing the monitoring process for hundreds of patients simultaneously. Concerns exist regarding false positives and ethical considerations related to the handling of brain data in both medical and consumer applications, emphasizing the need for responsible technological development. link
