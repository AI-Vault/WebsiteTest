OpenAI's recent release of GPT-5 has sparked significant backlash from users who perceive it as a downgrade compared to the previous model, GPT-4o. OpenAI CEO Sam Altman acknowledged these concerns, noting that a feature intended to switch between models failed, contributing to user dissatisfaction. Altman pledged to address these issues by enhancing GPT-5's performance and user experience. The Cleveland Clinic and startup Piramidal are collaborating to develop an AI model that utilizes electroencephalogram (EEG) data to monitor brain health in intensive care units (ICUs). The AI will analyze continuous EEG streams, enabling rapid detection of neurological issues such as seizures and altered consciousness. Currently, doctors rely on intermittent EEG monitoring, which can take hours to analyze. Piramidal’s model aims to provide real-time insights, significantly optimizing patient monitoring. Piramidal, founded in 2023 and backed by Y Combinator, has amassed nearly a million hours of EEG data to ensure its AI model can generalize brain activity patterns across diverse patients. Following initial testing, they plan gradual software rollout in ICUs. The initiative also raises ethical considerations regarding the use and storage of brain data, highlighting the necessity for frameworks to ensure responsible technology development in both medical and consumer applications. link
