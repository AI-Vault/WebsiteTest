An AI system significantly expedited the search and recovery of a hiker's body in Italy's Piedmont region. After Nicola Ivaldo, a doctor, went missing in September 2024, rescuers used drones equipped with image analysis software to comb a 183-hectare area. The operation, managed by the National Alpine and Speleological Rescue Corps (CNSAS), identified Ivaldo's helmet, which stood out in the landscape, just days after commencing the search. Utilizing AI, the team scanned 2,600 frames and pinpointed "suspicious spots" for further investigation within hours. This method minimized risks for rescuers and showcased the synergy between technology and human expertise in search and recovery operations. CNSAS aims to enhance this collaborative technology to reduce mountain accident fatalities effectively. link

OpenAI has released its first open-weight models since GPT-2: gpt-oss-120b and gpt-oss-20b. Benchmarks indicate strong performance, comparing favorably to OpenAI’s proprietary models.
