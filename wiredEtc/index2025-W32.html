An AI system enabled a mountain rescue team in Italy to locate the body of a missing hiker, Dr. Nicola Ivaldo, nearly a year after his disappearance. The National Alpine and Speleological Rescue Corps (CNSAS) utilized drones to gather 2,600 frames over a 183-hectare area. Traditional analysis would have taken weeks or longer, but AI reduced it to a mere afternoon, detecting color variations that indicated the presence of Ivaldo's helmet. The operation lasted less than three days, with initial image analysis yielding "suspicious spots" for further investigation. The success leverages AI's ability to analyze visual data rapidly, combined with expert human intervention. CNSAS emphasizes ongoing collaboration between technology and skilled rescuers to enhance safety in mountain rescues, aiming to reduce fatalities in such incidents. link
