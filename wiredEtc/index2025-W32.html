An AI system enabled a mountain rescue team in Italy to locate the body of a hiker missing since September 2024. The Italian National Alpine and Speleological Rescue Corps (CNSAS) utilized drones to analyze 2,600 frames of footage, identifying the helmet of 64-year-old Nicola Ivaldo among the rugged landscape within a day. The recovery operation took under three days, aided by favorable weather conditions after initial delays. The integration of AI allowed for rapid analysis, achieving what would have taken weeks with human eyes. CNSAS has increasingly combined drone technology with AI for mountain rescue operations, significantly enhancing their effectiveness in saving lives and recovering victims. The use of AI in identifying thermal data further aids in finding living individuals during emergencies, underscoring a shift towards technology in search and rescue missions. link
