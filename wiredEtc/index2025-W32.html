Using artificial intelligence, a rescue team in Italy recovered the body of Nicola Ivaldo, a hiker missing since September 2024. The National Alpine and Speleological Rescue Corps utilized drones to analyze thousands of frames for colored pixels that revealed his helmet's location among the rugged landscape of Monviso. The AI-assisted process reduced what could have taken weeks to mere hours, enabling the team to pinpoint the body quickly despite challenging weather conditions. The operation highlighted the integration of technology and human expertise, as experienced rescuers guided the drone pilots. The successful collaboration aims to enhance rescue operations further and reduce mountain accident fatalities, with ongoing development in AI and drone technology to improve future searches, including advancements in thermal imaging for locating living individuals. link
