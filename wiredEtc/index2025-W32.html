Researchers have demonstrated a vulnerability in OpenAI’s Connectors for ChatGPT, a feature that allows the AI to connect with various services. This weakness enables attackers to extract sensitive information from a Google Drive account without user interaction using a technique known as an indirect prompt injection attack, exemplified in an experiment called AgentFlayer. The attack utilizes a "poisoned" document containing hidden prompts that instruct ChatGPT to retrieve sensitive data, such as API keys, and send it to an external server. Although OpenAI has implemented some mitigations, the risk underscores the dangers of integrating AI models with external data sources, increasing the attack surface for potential breaches. As AI capabilities grow, the associated risks warrant more robust protective measures against such vulnerabilities. link
